# Skills, pearls and the shop (agreed with the owner, 7.5.7 → 7.6)

Everything here was agreed in conversation; built in the order at the end. Numbers are starting points, tuned in play.

## The stance (owner)

Skills are the heart of the game: magic you can see play out in the water is what makes progress feel earned. Pay to
win is fine and wanted; pearls are a fuel spent every day, not a rare premium. The design philosophy sets the quality
bar (painted, intentional, never generated), not limits on what the game may become (fantasy, magic, made-up animals).

## Slots

- Two scroll slots, switched with a swap chip on the skill button (as the net and the camera). The second slot is free
  from the start; empty, its chip is a greyed scroll with a padlock.
- Never two scrolls of one family. One Heart at a time: slot 1 takes up to mythical, slot 2 up to legendary; a mythical
  dragged to slot 2 is refused: "One Heart (mythical skill) at a time".
- Pressing a running skill again restarts it fresh; its effects do not carry over.

## Stacking: the strongest wins, from Earl's own base

Each kind of effect takes the strongest one running, never a product: speed (Sprint, Riptide, Maelstrom, the Hearts'
package, Gust, Plunge), light (Flare, Lighthouse, Pharos, the package, Lookout), reach (Vigor family), air refill rates
(Dauntless, Breath, the package), depth rating (Dauntless, Temper). Everything is measured from the diver's own base
(fins, suit, bag weight), never from a speed another skill gave.

## Charges

- Charges are loaded onto a scroll at the surface (anywhere at 0 m), bought with pearls: 1 pearl per 5 minutes of the
  scroll's cooldown. Each scroll holds its own; duplicates can be loaded and swapped in from the bag.
- Maximum per scroll: rare and exotic 5, legendary and mythical 10; set per scroll where different (Calm 3, Tidewarden
  and Warden Heart 1, Tidecall 2, Nacre 3, Ironskin 3, Bloodmoon 1).
- A charge fires the skill at once: no cooldown, no question. With no charges the normal cooldown runs free, or its
  confirm skips it at 3 pearls per 5 minutes left.
- Dashes keep their free charges per cooldown (2/2/3/5); loaded charges are whole cooldowns on top.
- A ring of segments round the skill button shows charges left (built for dashes in 7.5.6).

## The shop

- **Coins tab:** 50 pearls → $5,000; 100 → $15,000; 300 → $65,000; no daily limit; one daily deal (150 pearls →
  $30,000). The same for every player (never scaled by the hidden level).
- **Scroll shelf:** 3 scrolls, each on its own 12-hour clock; each roll rare 80 %, exotic 15 %, legendary 5 %; any
  family (hybrid, pure or special), pure ones a third as likely; never mythicals. Prices in pearls: hybrids 250 / 750 /
  2,000, pure 400 / 1,200 / 3,200, specials at the legendary price. "Restock now": 50 pearls rolls all three again.
- **Selling a scroll:** pearls only, a quarter of its shelf price (mythical 1,500), always confirmed.
- **Combine 3** shows on every scroll card that can combine, wherever it is shown; pure legendaries never combine.

## Kinds of family

- **Hybrid** (most): do a little more than one thing; mythicals (Hearts) are all hybrids with the Heart package (at
  least Flare+ light, Sprint+ speed, +50 % air over 10 s).
- **Pure** (rarer, a third as likely anywhere): one statistic, done better than any Heart in that lane; stop at
  legendary; a plain single-colour seal and a "Pure" mark on the card.
- **Creative**: for the fun of it, no edge in play.
- **Specials**: one rarity, few charges.
