# Item names that grow with rarity: proposal for approval

**Owner's decision (5.11.8): the Common and Exotic renames are applied; Uncommon, Rare and Legendary names stay as they were.** One change on applying: the common jet fins are *Hire jet fins*, as the uncommon *Jet fins* keeps its name. The three names used twice (*Steel 130, Sidemount pair, Light mesh bag*) are still open.

The proposal as it was put: Each item keeps its id, so saves are not affected; only the name shown changes.

**The rule**
- **Common**: plain, no maker. *Wetsuit, Hoop net, Canvas pouch.*
- **Uncommon**: a maker and a model. Shop stock carries the shop's own make (*Bayside jet fins, Dave's freediver skin, Station steel cylinder*); the rest keep the makers they have (*Tidewater Sport dip net*).
- **Rare**: a named line. Bayside's is *Pro*; what Dave sells came from other makers, so it carries their line (*Seabright Corsair carbon fins*); the Station numbers its kit by mark (*Mk III thermal drysuit*). The four sets (Harbour Patrol, Kelp Runner, Reef Keeper, Night Shift) already are named lines.
- **Exotic**: a history or an owner. Most already are (*Galathea expedition wetsuit, Kalymnos skafandro*); the plain ones get one.
- **Legendary**: dramatic. All already are (*Skin of the Old One, The Black Lung*); unchanged.

Also fixed: three names used twice (*Steel 130, Sidemount pair, Light mesh bag*).

**116 names change** of 393. Everything not listed keeps its name.
