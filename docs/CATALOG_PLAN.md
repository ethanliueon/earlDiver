# Shops, catalog and the Margaret's deck: plan v2 (proposed, not built)

Everything we have agreed so far, in one place. Items marked **(?)** still need a yes from the owner.

## The idea in one paragraph

The game says it is about collecting: catch reef and twilight animals, bring them up alive, sell them or keep
them in the gallery. The radio's quests stay in that shallow world. But the sea has more in it, and a curious
player finds it: a diver living in a hut far down the slope who sells better gear, and, at the bottom of it all,
the old anglerfish in her cave, who sells the best. Nothing points the way. There are no markers, only a few
low-key notes in wrecks.

## 1. The Margaret's deck (walking aboard)

- F at the stern ladder climbs aboard. The camera eases in until the deck fills the screen; A/D walk, W/S take the
  steps up to the wheelhouse. F at the ladder goes back into the water, and the camera eases out.
- Stations, each a small brass label and F:

| Station | What it does |
|---|---|
| Stern ladder | Into the water / aboard |
| Dive locker | Choose the gear for the dive (net, slurp gun, tag gun, camera, suit, fins, tank) and the bag size upgrade |
| Sorting table | Sell the catch (the receipt and the price board, as now); keep animals for the gallery |
| Sub cradle | The sub's upgrades (hull, engine, chambers, open tank, air rack), the claw (optional fit), patching |
| Radio | Quests |
| Computer | The Margaret's catalog |
| Helm | Steer the Margaret |
| Gallery door | The aquarium |

- The U shop popup goes away. A Tab menu jumps to any station for players who don't want to walk.
- New drawings: Earl standing and walking upright in each suit; the deck, wheelhouse and gallery door up close.

## 2. The radio: quests (text only, no voice)

- A short list of quests, each with progress and a reward to claim at the radio. For example: "Catch 10 reef
  fish 4/10 · $60", "Bring up a twilight animal alive · $200", "Keep 5 species in the gallery · a beginner net".
- New quests appear as old ones are done. They stay in the shallows and the twilight zone, and never say "go deep".
- The old random contracts are replaced by these quests.

## 3. Three shops

All prices in cash. Gold coins are removed.

### 3b. Dave's Haul: mid-game, found by exploring

- **Dave** is a real human diver who lives in an underwater hut on the seamount, about 1,200 m down: a pressurised
  habitat with a round porthole lit from inside, a hand-painted sign ("DAVE'S HAUL · OPEN"), nets, buoys and
  junk hanging outside.
- Earl swims or docks the sub at his airlock and presses F. Dave's shop opens: his own scruffy chalkboard look
  instead of the website.
- He sells rugged, deep-water gear:

## 4. The sub (decided)

- Hull, engine, chambers, open tank and air rack are bought at the sub cradle on deck.
- The light mast is built into every sub.
- The trawl net sits in the belly hatch, bought from the catalog (beginner) and also Dave (advanced).
- The claw is an optional fit at the cradle.

## 5. Clues (low key)

- A few things to find in wrecks and odd places: a note with a depth and a sketch of a hut, a lost camera with a
  photo of a lit porthole in the dark, a chart with a cave marked in the west wall. Each goes into the journal in
  Earl's words.
- They never grow louder; we can revisit later.

## 6. Order of building

1. The Margaret's deck: walking, stations, the close camera (today's screens behind each station at first).
2. The catalog on the computer, the dive locker, the items' rules (size range, specials); the tool, suit, fins and
   air ladders retired (old saves get the nearest items and the difference back).
3. Radio quests.
4. Dave's Haul: the hut, Dave, his shop.
5. The old one as a shop; gold coins removed.
6. Clues.
