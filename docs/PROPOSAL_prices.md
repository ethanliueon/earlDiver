# Prices on the new stats: proposal for approval

**Approved and applied in 5.12.9.** Chest prices are untouched (decided), and legendaries too (they only come from the old one and chests).

## The rule

Inside each rarity, an item's price follows how good it really is now: the weakest of its kind and rarity at the bottom of the band, the strongest near the top. What counts: seconds of air for tanks; speed and sprint for fins; pockets for bags; catch size, reach and success for nets; the camera's Quality; for suits the depth before pressure, the air saved (Rare up), and bite, cold and silence.

| Rarity | Your band | Prices would run |
|---|---|---|
| Common | $25 to $300 | $25 to $250 |
| Uncommon | $300 to $1,500 | $300 to $1,200 |
| Rare | $1,500 to $8,000 | $1,500 to $6,000 |
| Exotic | $8,000 to $40,000 | $8,000 to $30,000 |

This ends the flat prices (most rares were $1,800 whatever they did) and starts exotics at $8,000 instead of $15,000, so the step from rare to exotic is no longer 8 times.
